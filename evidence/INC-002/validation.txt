INC-002 - CPU Saturation
Evidence: Post-Mitigation Validation
Environment: Linux / WSL2

=== Mitigation ===

The controlled CPU workload was terminated using:

pkill yes

This command terminated the yes processes used to generate
the controlled CPU pressure.


=== CPU Validation ===

Command:

top

Observed after mitigation:

CPU idle: approximately 80.0%

Load average:

1 minute: 0.99
5 minutes: 0.99
15 minutes: 2.42


=== Baseline Comparison ===

Before the incident:

CPU idle: approximately 82.8%
1-minute load average: 0.59

During the incident:

CPU idle: 0.0%
1-minute load average: 5.12

After mitigation:

CPU idle: approximately 80.0%
1-minute load average: 0.99


=== Validation Result ===

After the controlled yes processes were terminated, CPU idle
increased from 0.0% to approximately 80.0%.

The 1-minute load average decreased from 5.12 during the
controlled incident to 0.99 after mitigation.

The measurements confirmed that CPU capacity became available
again after the controlled workload was removed.


=== Investigation Boundary ===

Separate I/O wait activity was observed in the environment after
the CPU experiment.

That activity was not attributed to the CPU saturation incident
because there was not sufficient evidence to establish that
relationship.
