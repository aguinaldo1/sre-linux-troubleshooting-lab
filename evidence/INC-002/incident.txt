INC-002 - CPU Saturation
Evidence: Incident
Environment: Linux / WSL2

=== Controlled CPU Workload ===

A controlled CPU workload was generated using the yes command.

Initial test:

yes > /dev/null

Observed:

Process: yes
PID: 49852
CPU usage: approximately 94.7%


=== CPU Saturation Test ===

Four yes processes were executed simultaneously to generate
CPU pressure across the available CPUs.

Observed during the controlled workload:

Available CPUs: 4

CPU idle: 0.0%

CPU distribution observed:

User CPU: approximately 13.5%
System CPU: approximately 51.0%
Nice CPU: approximately 35.4%


=== Load Average ===

Observed during CPU saturation:

1 minute: 5.12
5 minutes: 4.63
15 minutes: 3.05


=== Process-Level Evidence ===

The four controlled yes processes were observed consuming
approximately 88% to 95% CPU each.


=== Baseline Comparison ===

Before the controlled workload:

CPU idle: approximately 82.8%
1-minute load average: 0.59

During the controlled workload:

CPU idle: 0.0%
1-minute load average: 5.12


=== Evidence Correlation ===

The increase in load average occurred while the controlled
CPU-intensive processes were running.

CPU idle dropped from approximately 82.8% to 0.0%.

With 4 available CPUs and a 1-minute load average of 5.12,
the environment showed CPU demand exceeding the available
processing capacity during the experiment.
