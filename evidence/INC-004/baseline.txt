sre-lab-app.service

SreLabApp

/usr/bin/java -cp app SreLabApp

curl -i http://localhost:8080/health

Observed:

HTTP status: 200

Response body:

{"status":"ok"}

8080

/health

service as active and running.

on port 8080.

configuration to generate the controlled failure.
